Content Radar

A personal AI editorial assistant for one author's blog. It finds and sorts news and suggests ideas; the author decides what to write. It never publishes anything.

What it does

  • Collects new posts from public sources the author follows, such as public Telegram channels, and the articles they link to.
  • Once a day, uses an AI model to prepare a short digest of what the author should know and a few content idea cards. Each card includes a suggested angle, its reasoning, questions for the author and facts to check before publishing.
  • Delivers the digest and the cards to the author through a private Telegram bot, where the author marks which ideas are useful.
  • Leaves every decision to the author. Content Radar does not publish, post or send anything on the author's behalf.

Who it is for

Content Radar is a personal tool. It is built and used by its developer for their own blog and is not offered as a service to other people. There are no public sign-ups or user accounts.

Google Drive integration

The Google Drive integration lets the author move a promising idea from the Telegram bot into Google Drive with one button and keep working on it there:

  • When the author presses the “To editorial” button (“В редакцию”) on an idea card, Content Radar saves that idea's editorial brief as a Markdown file in an editorial folder in the author's own Google Drive and replies with a link to the file.
  • Content Radar asks for one permission only: drive.file. It gives access only to the files and folders that Content Radar itself creates. Content Radar cannot see, open or change any other file in Drive.
  • It does not request the author's name, email address, contacts or any other Google data.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Status: the Google Drive integration is in development and is not enabled yet. Content Radar has not been verified by Google.
