Content Radar prepares digests and content idea suggestions for the author. It never publishes, posts or sends anything on the author's behalf. All decisions about what to write and publish stay with the author.

3. AI-generated content

Digests, idea cards and editorial briefs are prepared with the help of AI models and may contain mistakes. They are drafts for the author's own consideration. Facts must be checked before anything is published.
